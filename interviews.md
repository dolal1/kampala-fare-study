# Rider interviews at stages

Aim for 5 to 10 short conversations with boda riders at stages near the study routes.
Ask permission, say it is for a personal study, and record no names or number plates.

## Questions

1. Which apps do you use, and which one do you open first? Why?
2. How many hours do you work on a normal day, and how many of your trips come from apps?
3. What makes you accept a trip request? What makes you reject or cancel one?
4. At what times or places are app trips not worth taking?
5. Since Uber left, has anything changed for you: requests, fares, bonuses?
6. What one change would make you take more trips on SafeBoda?
